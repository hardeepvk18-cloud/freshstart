Formula sheet

Only what these five papers actually needed.

Machining

V = πDN / 1000 (m/min),   N = 1000V / (πD)
D in mm, N in rpm. Use the diameter the question asks for — the diameter before the cut, or the mean diameter as in Oct 2025.
Number of passes = (D₁ − D₂) / (2 × depth of cut)
The depth of cut removes material from the radius, so the diameter drops by twice the depth each pass. Ø26 → Ø22 at 1 mm = 2 passes.
Machining time per pass = (L + pre-travel) / (f × N)
f × N is the feed rate in mm/min. Oct 2024: (150 + 10) / 100 = 1.6 min per pass, 3.2 min in total.
MRR = π × Dmean × d × f × N (mm³/min)
Dmean is the average of the diameters before and after that pass. Oct 2024 pass 1: π × 25 × 1 × 0.167 × 600 ≈ 7854 mm³/min.

Rolling

Draft d = h₀ − hf,   maximum draft dmax = μ²R
R is the roll radius — halve the diameter first. Mar 2025: μ = 0.15, R = 300 mm gives dmax = 6.75 mm.
Minimum friction μ = √(d / R)
Oct 2025: d = 15 mm, R = 300 mm gives μ ≈ 0.224.
Volume constancy: h₀w₀v₀ = hfwfvf
With widening, wf = w₀(1 + widening). Exit velocity vf = v₀h₀ / (hf × (1 + widening)).
Roll surface speed vr = 2πRN,   forward slip s = (vf − vr) / vr
Mar 2024: vr1 = 31.4 m/min, exit 34.2 m/min, s ≈ 0.089.

Sheet metal

Clearance per side c = a × t
a is the allowance, t the sheet thickness. Mar 2024: 0.064 × 1.6 = 0.1024 mm.
Blanking: die = blank size, punch = die − 2c · Punching: punch = hole size, die = punch + 2c
The part you keep sets the tool that matches it exactly.
Force F = (cut perimeter) × t × τ = πDtτ for a circle
Multiply by the number of holes. Mar 2024: blanking 88.5 kN, punching 44.7 kN.

Tools, chips & processes

Tool signature (ASA): back rake – side rake – end relief – side relief – end cutting edge angle – side cutting edge angle – nose radius
The relief (clearance) angles prevent rubbing between the tool and the work.
Positive rake vs. negative rake
Positive: lower cutting force and power, better finish, weaker edge — for soft, ductile materials. Negative: stronger edge, higher force and power — for hard or brittle materials and interrupted cuts.
Chip types
Continuous: ductile material, high speed, positive rake. Discontinuous: brittle material such as cast iron, low speed. Built-up edge (chip welding): ductile material at low speed with high friction — reduce it with higher speed, positive rake and coolant.
Forging by metal-flow constraint: open-die → impression-die (with flash) → flashless
Flash is the excess metal squeezed out between impression dies; flashless (closed-die) forging avoids it.
Thread rolling vs. thread cutting
Rolling is faster, wastes no material, and gives stronger threads because the grain flow follows the thread shape.
Additive manufacturing steps: CAD model → STL → slicing → layer-by-layer build → post-processing
FDM extrudes thermoplastic filament (e.g. PLA) through a heated nozzle and needs separate supports. SLS sinters polymer powder with a laser, and the loose powder supports the part.

Common mistakes that lose marks

Specific traps these five papers set — each one costs easy marks.

✕
Punch and die sizes swappedBlanking: die = blank size, punch smaller. Punching: punch = hole size, die bigger. Mar 2024, Oct 2024
✕
Applying the clearance once instead of twicec is per side, so the diameter changes by 2c. Mar 2024, Oct 2024
✕
Force for one hole onlyThe Oct 2024 machine punches two holes at once — double the force. Oct 2024
✕
Dividing the diameter difference by the depth of cutØ26 → Ø22 at 1 mm depth is 2 passes, not 4 — each pass takes 1 mm off the radius. Oct 2024, Mar 2026
✕
Same diameter for every passThe diameter shrinks after each pass, so cutting speed and MRR change pass by pass — unless the question says to use the mean diameter, as Oct 2025 did. Oct 2024, Mar 2025, Mar 2026
✕
Leaving out pre-travelAdd the approach length to the cut length before finding machining time. Oct 2024, Oct 2025
✕
Mixing feed unitsOct 2024 gave the feed in mm/min; Mar 2026 gave it in mm/rev. Convert with feed rate = f × N. Oct 2024, Mar 2026
✕
Using the roll diameter in d = μ²RR is the radius — Ø600 mm rolls mean R = 300 mm. Mar 2025, Oct 2025
✕
Taking the full maximum draft in the last passThe last pass only needs what's left: 2.625 mm in Mar 2025 and 4 mm in Mar 2026, not the full maximum. Mar 2025, Mar 2026
✕
Ignoring the wideningWidth grows every pass (6%, 4%, 5%), so exit velocity must include it. Mar 2025, Oct 2025, Mar 2026
✕
Mixing absolute and incremental coordinatesAbsolute is measured from the origin; incremental from the previous point. Mar 2026 required absolute mode. Mar 2025, Mar 2026
✕
Canned-cycle values in the wrong fieldsFollow the handout format exactly — for example, G71 U (depth) R (retract) on the first line, then P Q U W F. Oct 2024, Mar 2025, Oct 2025
✕
Tool signature in the wrong orderASA order: back rake, side rake, end relief, side relief, end cutting edge angle, side cutting edge angle, nose radius. Oct 2025, Mar 2026
✕
Skipping the schematicMany questions ask for a neat sketch or schematic — forging types, AM processes, rake angles, chip welding. All papers
