Formula sheet

Only the formulas these four papers actually required — each one checked against the standard form before being listed here.

Spectroscopy

A = ε · c · l
Beer-Lambert's Law. A = absorbance, ε = molar extinction coefficient (L·mol⁻¹cm⁻¹), c = concentration (mol/L), l = path length (cm). The single most-tested formula in this subject — appeared in all four papers.
A = log₁₀(I₀ / I) = log₁₀(1 / T)
Links absorbance to transmittance (T). Papers often give %T and expect a conversion (T = %T / 100) before applying Beer-Lambert.
% Absorption = 100 − % Transmittance
When a question asks for "% absorption" (not absorbance), this is the final step. Oct 2024 pattern: find A from %T, scale A with concentration (A ∝ c), convert back to %T, then subtract from 100.
% Absorption = 100 − % Transmittance
The simple version used when a question asks for "% absorption" directly from a transmittance value, rather than the full absorbance (A). Don't confuse this with the log-based formula above — papers use both, in different questions.
c = ν · λ
Speed of light = frequency × wavelength. Needed whenever a question gives a frequency (e.g. a broadcast frequency in kHz/Hz) and asks for the corresponding wavelength.
c = ν · λ  →  λ = c / ν
Speed of light = frequency × wavelength (c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s). Needed for the Oct 2024 radio-station question (1368 kHz → wavelength). Convert kHz to Hz first (× 10³).
E = hc / λ = hν
Energy of a photon. Used for energy-gap calculations, e.g. the sodium-atom absorption question.
N* / N₀ = (g* / g₀) · e^(−ΔE / k_BT)
Boltzmann population ratio between excited and ground states — paired with the energy-gap formula above.
ν̃ = (1 / 2πc) · √(k / μ),   μ = m₁m₂ / (m₁+m₂)
IR vibrational wavenumber (Hooke's Law model). μ is the reduced mass in kg. Use c = 3 × 10¹⁰ cm/s (not m/s) so the answer comes out in cm⁻¹. Check: the Oct 2024 H³⁵Cl data gives ≈ 2885 cm⁻¹.
Vibrational modes: 3N − 5 (linear), 3N − 6 (non-linear)
Asked directly for CO₂, H₂O, and acetylene. Confirm whether the molecule is linear before picking the formula.

Water Chemistry

Hardness (ppm as CaCO₃) = (VEDTA × MEDTA × 100 × 1000) / Vsample
The 100 comes from the molar mass of CaCO₃ (g/mol); the 1000 converts grams to milligrams. Every EDTA question in these papers uses this exact structure — raw sample first, boiled sample second.
Temporary hardness = Total hardness − Permanent hardness
Total comes from the unboiled sample's titration, permanent from the boiled sample's. This subtraction is easy, guaranteed marks — don't skip writing it out.

Polymers

M̄n = ΣNᵢMᵢ / ΣNᵢ
Number-average molecular weight.
M̄w = ΣNᵢMᵢ² / ΣNᵢMᵢ
Weight-average molecular weight — note the extra power of Mᵢ compared to M̄n.
PDI = M̄w / M̄n
Polydispersity index. Always ≥ 1; asked directly after the two averages above in the Oct 2025 paper.

Common mistakes that lose marks

Specific traps these four papers set — each one costs easy marks.

✕
Putting %T straight into the log60% transmittance means T = 0.60. Convert first, then use A = log(1/T). Oct 2024, Mar 2025
✕
Confusing absorbance with % absorption"% absorption" = 100 − %T. It is not A × 100. Oct 2024, Oct 2025
✕
Wrong concentration units2.1 mM = 2.1 × 10⁻³ M. ε in L mol⁻¹ cm⁻¹ needs c in mol/L and l in cm. Mar 2024
✕
Assuming Beer-Lambert works for white lightThe law holds only for monochromatic light, so the Oct 2025 statement is false. Oct 2025
✕
Getting phosphorescence backwardsPhosphorescence comes from the lower triplet state, so it is at a longer wavelength and is weaker than fluorescence — the Mar 2025 statement is false. Mar 2025
✕
Saying fluorescence matches the absorbed wavelengthEmission happens from S₁ even after S₀→S₂ absorption, so fluorescence is at a longer wavelength. Mar 2024
✕
Linear vs. non-linear in 3N − 5 / 3N − 6CO₂ and acetylene are linear (4 and 7 modes); H₂O is non-linear (3 modes). Mar 2024, Oct 2024
✕
IR wavenumber in the wrong unitUsing c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s gives m⁻¹, not cm⁻¹. Use 3 × 10¹⁰ cm/s. Oct 2024
✕
Skipping or reversing the boiled-sample stepTotal hardness comes from the unboiled sample, permanent from the boiled one; temporary = total − permanent. Mar 2024, Oct 2025
✕
Mixing up hardness and alkalinityCaCl₂ and MgSO₄ cause hardness, not alkalinity. Alkalinity comes from HCO₃⁻, CO₃²⁻ and OH⁻ — so Mg(HCO₃)₂ and KOH. Oct 2025
✕
Saying every polymer shows tacticityTacticity needs an asymmetric carbon in the repeat unit — one carrying two different groups, like the –C₆H₅ side group in polystyrene. Polyethylene (all H) and PTFE (all F) have none, so they show no tacticity. Oct 2025
✕
Swapping M̄n and M̄wM̄w has Mᵢ² in the numerator. PDI = M̄w/M̄n is always ≥ 1 — if you get less than 1, you've swapped them. Oct 2025
✕
Unlabelled Jablonski diagramName every process, and draw internal conversion and intersystem crossing as non-radiative (wavy) arrows. Mar 2024, Oct 2024

If you only have one evening

  1. Beer-Lambert's Law numerical — practice 3-4 variations (given A find c, given %T find ε, etc). Appeared in 4/4 papers.
  2. AAS / AES / ICP-AES comparison table — memorise as a table, not paragraphs.
  3. Water hardness (EDTA) numerical — the two-titration structure, both formulas.
  4. Jablonski diagram — practice drawing it once, fully labelled, from memory.
  5. If time is left: chromophore/auxochrome definitions with one example each (benzene, phenol, aniline keep showing up), and the IR-active criterion.
