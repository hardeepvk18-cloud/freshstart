Formula sheet

Only what these three papers actually needed.

Differential equations

Reduction of order: y₂ = y₁ ∫ e−∫P dx / y₁² dx
First divide by the coefficient of y″ to get y″ + P y′ + Q y = 0. For March 2025 this gives y₂ = x² + x + 2 (checked).
Cauchy–Euler: x = ez, D = d/dz,   x y′ = D y,   x² y″ = D(D − 1) y
After solving in z, replace z by ln x. On the right side, ln x becomes z and xⁿ becomes enz.
Legendre: a + bx = ez,   (a + bx) y′ = b D y,   (a + bx)² y″ = b² D(D − 1) y
For (1 − x), b = −1: (1 − x) y′ = −D y and (1 − x)² y″ = D(D − 1) y.
Operator method: (1/f(D)) eaz V = eaz (1/f(D + a)) V
If f(a) ≠ 0, (1/f(D)) eaz = eaz/f(a). For a polynomial in z, expand 1/f(D) as a series in D.
LCR: L q″ + R q′ + q/C = E(t),   i = dq/dt
With L = 0.1, R = 0.3, C = 5 this becomes q″ + 3q′ + 2q = 10E(t). For E = cos t with q(0) = q′(0) = 0: q = 3 sin t + cos t − 5e−t + 4e−2t (checked).
Bernoulli: y′ + Py = Qyⁿ → v = y1−n (March 2024 only)
For xy²y′ + y³ = x cos x, v = y³ makes it linear, since v′ = 3y²y′.

Laplace transforms

L{1} = 1/s,   L{tⁿ} = n!/sn+1,   L{eat} = 1/(s − a),   L{sin bt} = b/(s² + b²),   L{cos bt} = s/(s² + b²)
The standard table.
First shifting: L{eat f(t)} = F(s − a)
So e−2t means replace s by s + 2.
Multiplication by t: L{t f(t)} = −F′(s)
Keep the minus sign.
Division by t: L{f(t)/t} = ∫ₛ^∞ F(u) du
E.g. L{(1 − cos t)/t} = ½ ln((s² + 1)/s²) (checked numerically).
L{f′} = sF(s) − f(0),   L{f″} = s²F(s) − s f(0) − f′(0)
The basis of every IVP.
L{∫₀ᵗ f(u) du} = F(s)/s
Used in the March 2026 integral.
Second shifting: L{f(t − a) u(t − a)} = e−as F(s)
Piecewise: f = f₁ + (f₂ − f₁) u(t − a). Rewrite f₂ in terms of (t − a) first, e.g. e−t = e−1·e−(t−1).
∫₀^∞ e−st f(t) dt = F(s) at the given s,   ∫₀^∞ f(t)/t dt = ∫₀^∞ F(u) du
∫₀^∞ t e−3t cos t dt = 2/25; ∫₀^∞ e−3t sin²t / t dt = ¼ ln(13/9); the March 2026 integral = ln(7/4) (all checked).
Inverse of a log: f(t) = −(1/t) · L⁻¹{F′(s)}
L⁻¹{ln((s + 3)/(s + 4))} = (e−4t − e−3t)/t; L⁻¹{ln((s² + 1)/(s² + 4))} = 2(cos 2t − cos t)/t (checked).
Convolution: L⁻¹{F(s)G(s)} = ∫₀ᵗ f(u) g(t − u) du
L⁻¹{s/(s² + 4)²} = (t sin 2t)/4; L⁻¹{1/(s²(s² + 9))} = t/9 − (sin 3t)/27 (checked).
sin A cos B = ½[sin(A + B) + sin(A − B)]
Turn trig products into sums before transforming.

Fourier series (March 2024 only)

f(x) ~ a₀/2 + Σ (aₙ cos nx + bₙ sin nx)
aₙ = (1/π) ∫−ππ f cos nx dx, bₙ = (1/π) ∫−ππ f sin nx dx, and a₀ = (1/π) ∫−ππ f dx.

Common mistakes that lose marks

Specific traps these three papers set — each one costs easy marks.

✕
Not checking the given solutionAs printed, y₁ = x + 1 does not satisfy the March 2026 equation — it leaves −4(x + 1). If a given solution doesn't check out, say so and state the assumption you're making. Mar 2026
✕
Skipping standard form in reduction of orderP is the coefficient of y′ only after dividing the whole equation by the coefficient of y″. Mar 2025, Mar 2026
✕
x²y″ = D²yIt's D(D − 1)y. Only xy′ becomes Dy. All papers
✕
Dropping the sign in a Legendre equationWith (1 − x), (1 − x)y′ = −Dy. Mar 2024
✕
Leaving the answer in zAfter a Cauchy–Euler substitution, convert back: z = ln x and ez = x. Mar 2025, Mar 2026
✕
Wrong LCR equationDivide through correctly: with L = 0.1, R = 0.3, C = 5 it's q″ + 3q′ + 2q = 10E(t), and both q(0) and q′(0) are zero. Mar 2025, Mar 2026
✕
Shifting the wrong waye−2t f(t) gives F(s + 2), not F(s − 2). Mar 2025, Mar 2026
✕
Losing the minus in L{t f(t)} = −F′(s)It changes the sign of the whole answer. All papers
✕
Second shifting without rewriting fe−tu(t − 1) must first become e−1e−(t−1)u(t − 1) before using e−asF(s). Mar 2025, Mar 2026
✕
Forgetting initial values in L{y″}L{y″} = s²Y − s y(0) − y′(0); leaving out y(0) or y′(0) breaks the whole IVP. Mar 2024, Mar 2026
✕
Inverting a log directlyDifferentiate F(s) first, invert that, then divide by −t. Mar 2025, Mar 2026
✕
Not splitting the numerator after completing the squares² + 2s + 17 = (s + 1)² + 16, so write 3s − 8 as 3(s + 1) − 11 before inverting. Mar 2025
✕
Convolution with the wrong argumentIt's ∫₀ᵗ f(u) g(t − u) du — one factor must use (t − u). Mar 2024, Mar 2026
