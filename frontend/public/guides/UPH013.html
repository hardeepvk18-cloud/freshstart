Formula sheet

Only what these four papers actually needed.

Oscillations

m ẍ + b ẋ + k x = 0  →  ẍ + 2β ẋ + ω₀² x = 0,   β = b/2m,   ω₀ = √(k/m)
Under-damped: β < ω₀, oscillates with ω′ = √(ω₀² − β²). Critically damped: β = ω₀. Over-damped: β > ω₀, no oscillation.
LCR: L q̈ + R q̇ + q/C = 0,   β = R/2L,   ω₀ = 1/√(LC)
Under-damped: R² < 4L/C. Critical: R² = 4L/C. Over-damped: R² > 4L/C. Analogues: m ↔ L, b ↔ R, k ↔ 1/C, x ↔ q.
SHM: T = 2π√(m/k),   x = A cos ωt,   v = −Aω sin ωt
Released from rest at displacement A. ωt is in radians — Oct 2025 gives x = −0.0495 m and speed 0.035 m/s at t = 0.6 s.
E = ½kA²,   KE at x = ½k(A² − x²),   speed = ω√(A² − x²)
For a vertical spring, measure x from the new equilibrium position.

Acoustics

Sabine: T = 0.161 V / A (SI),   T = 0.049 V / A (FPS)
V in m³ or ft³, A = Σ αᵢSᵢ = ᾱS in m² sabin or ft² O.W.U. Three papers used feet, Oct 2025 used metres.
Room surface area S = 2(lb + bh + hl)
Hemisphere of radius r: V = ⅔πr³, curved surface 2πr², flat floor πr².
Eyring: T = 0.161 V / [−S ln(1 − ᾱ)]
Accurate at any absorption; reduces to Sabine when ᾱ is small. As ᾱ → 1 Eyring gives T → 0, while Sabine still gives a finite time — which is why Eyring is considered better.

Ultrasonics

Magnetostriction: f = (1/2l)√(Y/ρ),   Piezoelectric: f = (1/2t)√(Y/ρ)
l is the rod length, t the crystal thickness. For the Mar 2025 data, l ≈ 0.90 t.
Nodal planes are λ/2 apart, so v = fλ = 2fd
Mar 2026: v = 2 × 2.5 × 10⁶ × 0.29 × 10⁻³ = 1450 m/s.

Electromagnetism

Free space: ∇·E = 0,   ∇·B = 0,   ∇×E = −∂B/∂t,   ∇×B = μ₀ε₀ ∂E/∂t
In a conducting medium: ∇·D = ρ, ∇·B = 0, ∇×E = −∂B/∂t, ∇×H = J + ∂D/∂t, with J = σE.
∇²E = μ₀ε₀ ∂²E/∂t²,   v = 1/√(μ₀ε₀) = 3 × 10⁸ m/s
Take the curl of the curl equation and use ∇×(∇×E) = ∇(∇·E) − ∇²E.
Conducting medium: ∇²E = μσ ∂E/∂t + με ∂²E/∂t²
The σ term makes k² complex, so the wave is attenuated.
Good conductor: σ / (ωε) ≫ 1
Doped silicon at 10⁸ Hz gives σ/(ωε) ≈ 30 — a good conductor, and more so at lower frequencies.
Skin depth: δ = √(2 / (μσω)) = 1 / √(πfμσ)
μ = μ₀μᵣ, ω = 2πf. Copper at 10 MHz: δ ≈ 20.9 µm. Doped silicon at 10⁶ Hz: δ ≈ 0.356 m.
Displacement current density: Jd = ε₀ ∂E/∂t
It exists only when E changes with time.
∇f = (∂f/∂x, ∂f/∂y, ∂f/∂z),   ∇·A = ∂Aₓ/∂x + ∂A_y/∂y + ∂A_z/∂z
Curl is the determinant of (î ĵ k̂; ∂/∂x ∂/∂y ∂/∂z; Aₓ A_y A_z). Zero curl means an irrotational, conservative field — as in Oct 2025.

Optics (Oct 2025 only)

Reflected light in a thin film: bright when 2μt cos r = (2n + 1)λ/2, dark when 2μt cos r = nλ
The extra λ/2 comes from the phase change on reflection at the denser surface.

Common mistakes that lose marks

Specific traps these four papers set — each one costs easy marks.

✕
Calculator in degreesωt = 5 × 0.6 = 3 is in radians. In degree mode you'd get x ≈ 0.0499 m instead of −0.0495 m. Oct 2025
✕
Wrong amplitude"Stretched further by 2 cm and released" means A = 2 cm, and "pulled 10 cm below and released" means A = 10 cm. Oct 2024, Mar 2026
✕
Flipping the frequency ratioNatural frequency = (2/√3) × damped frequency, so ω′ = (√3/2)ω₀. Then β = √(ω₀² − ω′²) and b = 2mβ. Mar 2025
✕
Wrong damping condition for LCRIt's R² compared with 4L/C, not R compared with 4L/C. Oct 2024, Mar 2025, Oct 2025
✕
Wrong constant in Sabine's formula0.049 for feet, 0.161 for metres. Three papers used feet, one used metres. All papers
✕
Missing faces of the roomA box room has six faces: S = 2(lb + bh + hl). Mar 2025, Mar 2026
✕
Not stating whether the floor countsFor the dome hall, counting the floor gives T ≈ 1.79 s and leaving it out gives ≈ 2.68 s. State your assumption. Oct 2025
✕
Taking node spacing as λConsecutive nodal planes are λ/2 apart, so v = 2fd. Mar 2026
✕
Using f instead of ωSkin depth and the good-conductor test use ω = 2πf, and μ = μ₀μᵣ. Oct 2025, Mar 2026
✕
Mixing free-space and conducting equationsIn free space ρ = 0 and J = 0. In a conductor, keep J = σE. All papers
✕
Forgetting the λ/2 phase changeLeaving it out swaps the bright and dark conditions for reflected light. Oct 2025
✕
Skipping the diagramUltrasonic generators, reverberation time and damping curves were all asked "with a diagram" or "graph". Mar 2025, Oct 2025, Mar 2026
✕
True/false without justificationEach true/false question asked you to justify — the reason carries the mark. Oct 2024

If you only have one evening

  1. Maxwell's equations — free space and conducting medium, the wave equation, and v = c.
  2. Damped oscillator and LCR derivation — with the three damping conditions and graphs.
  3. One SHM numerical of each type — period, energy, position and speed at time t, and damping from a frequency ratio.
  4. Ultrasonic generators — magnetostriction and piezoelectric, each with a labelled diagram.
  5. Reverberation — Sabine in feet and metres, extra absorption, and Sabine vs. Eyring.
  6. If time is left: skin depth, grad/div/curl, and thin-film interference.
